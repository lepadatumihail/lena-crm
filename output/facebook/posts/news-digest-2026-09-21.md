# Costa del Sol property, the week in five lines (draft, week of 14–20 Sep 2026)

Costa del Sol property, this week in five lines:

1. Vélez-Málaga is now the most in-demand rental market in the province. Asking rents there are €11.20/m² against €16.50/m² in Málaga city, and house-hunters priced out of the city are heading east. (Idealista data, reported by The Olive Press)

2. The cheapest places to buy in Málaga province are inland: Humilladero at €903/m², Alameda €917, Tolox €1,015, Ardales €1,128. (Idealista, July figures)

3. Marbella town hall has announced 172 protected rental homes in San Pedro Alcántara, one to four bedrooms from €800 a month, for people on the housing register. (Europa Press, La Opinión de Málaga)

4. Dils Lucas Fox has opened a Málaga city office on the Paseo Marítimo Pablo Ruiz Picasso. (Sur in English, 101TV)

5. Málaga's moratorium on new hotels and tourist flats is being fought by the hotel sector. (Málaga Hoy, Diario Sur)

If your buyers say Málaga city is out of reach, this is where the data says they go next.
