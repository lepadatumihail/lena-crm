- Everything is published on the LeadWing page only. No personal or group posts.

Send the key as `Authorization: Bearer`. At most 10 searches per run; check `GET https://www.searchapi.io/api/v1/me` and judge by `current_month_usage` (allowance 10,000; stay under 400 a month).
- Keep: market data, prices and rents, regulation and licences, new developments, infrastructure, agency and industry moves. Skip: crime, celebrities, portal listing pages, national stories without a coast angle, stories already used (check the log).

## Learnings (append, dated)
- 20 Sep 2026: Audience Network delivered 42k impressions and 165 "clicks" for €5 with 0 sign-ups; never leave placements automatic on a link-click ad set without a pixel. A Meta pixel on the site would let ads optimise for landing page views.
- 20 Sep 2026 (late run): taking Audience Network away did not move the money to the feed — it stopped delivery almost entirely. The ad set is LOWEST_COST_WITHOUT_CAP with standard pacing on a €25 lifetime budget, so with four days left it only needs ~€4.80/day and will not bid up for a narrow feed audience (60 km of Marbella, agent job titles + interests, 24–64). Cheap junk inventory was what made the budget "work". Expect feed CPM of €5–15, i.e. €19.36 buys roughly 1,300–3,900 real feed impressions, not 40,000.
- 20 Sep 2026: `render.mjs` had the founder's Mac path hardcoded, so it could not run in a cloud checkout; it now derives the repo root from its own location and falls back to `SHARP_DIR` for sharp. `npm ci` fails in this repo (package-lock.json is out of sync, missing @swc/helpers) — install sharp elsewhere and set `SHARP_DIR` rather than running `npm install`, which would rewrite the lockfile.
- 20 Sep 2026: SearchAPI reports `remaining_credits` separately from the 10,000 monthly allowance — it was down to 65 with only 274 searches used. Judge by `remaining_credits`, not `current_month_usage`.
- 23 Sep 2026: sign-ups are **not readable from the marketing session** — do not keep trying. The Vercel connector reaches the `lena-crm` project (prj_q2AubcSbz6BCdQDAw4PpR5nLf0Aa, team_uvdnEdq0a8yc2ngH0P2S84m5) but `aggregate_pageviews` returns 404 "Web Analytics not found", and the session's environment carries no DATABASE_URL or any app credential — only the Meta, SearchAPI and OpenAI keys. The repo has `@vercel/analytics` but no PostHog. So the operator's funnel stops at Meta's `landing_page_view`; every number past that (sign-in attempts, sign-ups, Founding Agents) is the founder's read in the auth log. Report landing views and say plainly that the rest is unmeasured, rather than implying zero.
- 23 Sep 2026: this cloud environment's network policy blocks every news site (theolivepress.es, laopiniondemalaga.es, larazon.es, elconciso.es, idealista.com, europapress.es, diariosur.es all refused, WebFetch and curl alike). The WebSearch tool still works and returns the article text server-side, so verify each figure that way against at least two independent sources, and drop any number only one source carries. Say in the digest file that articles were not opened directly. The founder can widen the environment's network policy to restore direct fetches.
- 23 Sep 2026: `compose-product.mjs` upper-cases the pill text, so an `&amp;` becomes `&AMP;` and Sharp rejects the markup. Keep ampersands out of pills. OpenAI backgrounds come out too light for ivory text; darken them with Sharp `modulate({brightness: 0.6})` before composing.
- 21 Sep 2026: the site's Meta Pixel (src/components/marketing/meta-pixel.tsx) loads only after the visitor accepts the cookie notice (src/components/marketing/marketing-tracking.tsx) and only on marketing URLs; the answer lives in localStorage `lw_consent` for a year, the footer's Cookies link reopens the notice, and the privacy policy (§8, EN + ES) describes it. Without NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID nothing renders. Consent is required in Spain, so expect the pixel to see a fraction of visits, not all of them.
- 21 Sep 2026 (evening): REACH was the right lever — it bought feed inventory outright within an hour of the edit, €3.23 for 4,089 feed impressions at a €0.79 CPM, no Audience Network at all. Real feed clicks stay rare (4 clicks, CTR 0.10%, ~€0.81 each): REACH buys eyeballs, not clicks, so judge it on reach and CPM and do not expect the click numbers a junk-inventory run produced.
- 21 Sep 2026 (evening): the waste test reads lifetime figures, so it stays tripped by spend that a fix already stopped — lifetime AN share was still 58% and lifetime cost per click €0.051 while the live ad had zero AN and €0.81 clicks. Before pausing, check whether the offending spend predates the fix and what today's own numbers say; pausing on a stale lifetime average would only freeze a healthy ad.
- 21 Sep 2026: for an OUTCOME_TRAFFIC ad set, LANDING_PAGE_VIEWS takes no `promoted_object`; passing `{pixel_id}` alone fails with "Promoted Object Invalid" (subcode 1885014). The account's pixel is matched to the link URL automatically.
- 22 Sep 2026: LANDING_PAGE_VIEWS is the goal that actually works here, better than both LINK_CLICKS (no delivery) and REACH (delivery but no clicks). In the 4.5 h after the 21 Sep 19:35 switch it spent €4.77 and produced 17 clicks and 9 landing page views, and on 22 Sep landing views ran at 64% of clicks (€0.23 per landing view) against 22% on Audience Network junk. The pixel is what made it possible — without it Meta cannot optimise for the thing worth buying.
- 21 Sep 2026: compressing the lifetime budget into fewer days did not restore delivery either — a full day at zero impressions and zero spend. Feed-only + a narrow interest/job-title audience + LOWEST_COST_WITHOUT_CAP + LINK_CLICKS with no pixel simply loses the auction; the lever is the optimization goal (REACH buys feed inventory outright) or audience width, not the pacing window. An ad set with no delivery returns no insights row for that day at all, so "no row" means zero, not missing data.
