# Costa del Sol property, the week in five lines (draft for Fri 25 Sep 2026 09:30, week of 21–27 Sep)

Card: `08-digest-2026-09-25.png`

Costa del Sol property, this week in five lines:

1. Marbella has approved its first new town plan since 1986. It aims to regularise around 18,000 homes left outside the rules by the old planning mess, and reserves 20% of building capacity on urban land for affordable housing. (El Español, Marbella town hall)

2. Appraised home values in Andalucía hit a record €1,981.6/m², up 13.8% in a year and now well above the 2008 peak of €1,805.8. Málaga remains the dearest province in the region. (Ministry of Housing data, via El Conciso and El Español)

3. Renting an average 90 m² flat in Málaga city now takes 38% of household income, against the 33% experts treat as the limit. Only Madrid is more stretched, at 40%. (Century 21 access-to-housing report, via Andalucía Información)

4. Registration for 25 protected homes on Marbella's Golden Mile closes on 1 October. Prices run from €180,000 to just under €280,000, and the draw is expected at the end of the year or early 2027. (El Periódico de Marbella, Málaga Press)

5. Málaga's mayor suggested that foreign buyers pushing up prices should "compensate economically", and confirmed the city is not applying a stressed-zone rent cap because the Junta does not support one. (El Español, Nueva Economía Fórum)

If you have Marbella sellers whose paperwork has been a sticking point, now is a good week to call them.
