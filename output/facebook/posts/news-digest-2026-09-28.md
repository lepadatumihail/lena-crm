# Costa del Sol property, the week in five lines (draft for Mon 28 Sep 2026 09:30, week of 21–27 Sep)

Card: `11-digest-2026-09-28.png`

Costa del Sol property, this week in five lines:

1. Andalucía is cutting the transfer tax on resale homes from 2027. The general ITP rate drops from 7% to 6.75%, with a quarter point a year planned until it reaches 6%. (Junta de Andalucía 2027 budget, via The Objective and La Voz del Sur)

2. In the same change, the price ceiling for the reduced 3.5% rate rises from €150,000 to €200,000 — €300,000 for large families and buyers with a disability. It applies to under-35s and other priority groups, and the Junta expects around 146,500 Andalusians to benefit. (Junta figures, via Euro Weekly News and The Objective)

3. Benahavís averaged €5,553/m² in August, keeping the Marbella side of the coast at the top end of the national market. (idealista, Just Real Estate index)

4. Málaga city has signed off around 100 tourist apartments in seven buildings this summer despite the moratorium that began on 25 July, because files submitted complete before that date keep moving. The freeze itself runs three years, to 2029. (Moncloa, Teleprensa)

5. Spain's market has split in two: coastal Málaga keeps getting harder to afford while inland stays cheap — Humilladero is the province's least expensive municipality at €903/m². (idealista)

For buyers sitting on a resale under €200,000, the arithmetic changes in January, and that is a reason to call them now rather than in December.
