# Outreach follow-up drafts — 26 Sep 2026

Context: the founder sent 20 of the 50 sheet rows. 15 no answer, 5 "not interested", 0 interested.
Nothing here has been sent. Sending waits on the founder's explicit yes.

## Send 1 — to the 5 who said "not interested" (the valuable one)

One question, no pitch, no link. The point is to learn what they use today, which decides
whether this is a copy problem or a positioning problem.

**EN**

> Thanks for answering straight — that's useful on its own. Can I ask one thing? What do you
> use today for your buyer follow-ups? I'm building this for agents here on the coast, and I'd
> rather understand what you actually need than sell you something that doesn't fit.
> — Mihail

**ES**

> Gracias por responder con claridad, me sirve. ¿Te puedo preguntar una cosa? ¿Qué usas ahora
> para el seguimiento de tus compradores? Estoy construyendo esto para agentes de la costa y
> prefiero entender qué necesitáis de verdad antes que venderte algo que no te encaja.
> — Mihail

Why this wording: no offer, no price, no URL, nothing to decline. A person who has already said
no can answer this in four words, and each answer tells us something different — "Inmovilla",
"Resales", "Excel and it's fine", "too expensive", "no time".

## What to log per reply

For each answer, record in the sheet's Status column: the tool they name (if any), the reason
given, and whether they manage buyers in Excel/WhatsApp. Five named tools would tell us more
about what to build next than another 30 sends.
