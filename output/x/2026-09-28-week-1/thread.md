**Post 1** + `01-hook.png`

My CRM for real-estate agents has been stuck at 10 users for 3 weeks.

So I handed my Facebook ads to a Claude agent. It checks everything twice a day, drafts the posts, and needs my yes before it publishes or spends anything.

Final: €45 → 154 landing page views → 0 sign-ups.

What it found was worth more than the ads 🧵

**Post 2** + `02-placements.png`

1. Meta's automatic placements sent 89% of my first €5.64 to Audience Network, ads inside random third-party apps.

165 "clicks". 4 came from the real feeds.

Running a link ad without a pixel? Feeds only.

---

**Post 3** + `03-audience.png`

2. Meta can't target real-estate agents in Spain.

The agent job titles return Meta's minimum of 1,000 people for the whole country, so almost nobody has them filled in. My "agent audience" was really ~35k people nearby who are interested in property. Consumers, not agents.

---

**Post 4** + `04-agent-said-no.png`

3. It pushed back on me.

I approved widening the audience by dropping those job titles. It checked Meta's estimate first: the titles were OR'd with the interests, so dropping them changed the audience by 0.3%.

It didn't apply my change. It was right.

---

**Post 5** + `05-outreach.png`

4. Meanwhile, by hand: I messaged 20 local agencies.

15 didn't answer. 5 said not interested. 0 interested.

My working guess: agents here already pay for listing software with a CRM built in. No ad budget fixes that.
