## Part 2 — Requirements checklist (primary sources)

1. **Aviso legal, LSSI-CE art. 10** (boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758): name, address/establishment in Spain, email plus another direct contact, NIF, Registro Mercantil data "en su caso" (autónomos are not registered: omit), whether prices include taxes ("IVA no incluido"). Electronic contracting art. 27: steps, whether the contract is archived, how to correct input errors, contract language (waivable B2B); art. 28 acknowledge acceptance within 24 h; art. 21: no marketing email without consent except own similar services to existing customers with opt-out. Do not link the EU ODR platform (discontinued 20 Jul 2025).
2. **Privacy policy, GDPR arts. 13/14 + LOPDGDD**: controller identity, DPO if any, purposes + legal basis with legitimate interests spelled out, recipients, transfers + safeguard, retention criteria, rights incl. withdrawal and AEPD complaint, whether provision is contractual, automated decisions. LOPDGDD art. 11 two layers; AEPD first-layer table Responsable / Finalidad / Legitimación / Destinatarios / Derechos / Información adicional (aepd.es/guias/guia-modelo-clausula-informativa.pdf). Art. 14 toward buyers/sellers is the agent's duty; LeadWing as processor refers them back. Art. 32: block deleted data during limitation periods. DPO not mandatory for a small CRM. Google OAuth: consent screen and homepage must link a privacy policy on leadwing.app that explains Google-data use (support.google.com/cloud/answer/13464321).
3. **Cookies, LSSI art. 22.2 + AEPD Guía de cookies (May 2024, aepd.es/guias/guia-cookies.pdf)**: authentication, security, user-input and language-preference cookies are exempt; if every cookie is exempt no banner or consent is needed, but the AEPD recommends disclosing them generically (done in the privacy policy §8). Analytics cookies are not exempt unless first-party, purely statistical, ≤13 months (aepd.es/guias/guia-cookies-analiticas-externas.pdf). Vercel Web Analytics stores nothing on the device and discards its hash within 24 h (vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy): no consent, still disclose. If a banner is ever added, "reject" must sit at the same level as "accept".
4. **Art. 28 DPA** (AEPD directrices: aepd.es/guias/guia-directrices-contratos.pdf; EDPB Guidelines 07/2020; template Decision (EU) 2021/915): subject matter, duration, nature/purpose, data types (incl. ID scans), data-subject categories, plus (a) documented instructions incl. transfers, (b) confidentiality, (c) art. 32 security, (d) sub-processors with notice + objection and flow-down, (e) assist with rights, (f) assist with security/breach/DPIA, (g) delete or return at end with form and deadline, (h) information and audits, flag unlawful instructions. Processor may keep a blocked copy while liabilities can arise. Own-purpose use (AI training, product analytics on customer data) turns the processor into a controller.
5. **Stripe** (docs.stripe.com/get-started/checklist/website): business name, description of services, currency, customer-service contact beyond a form, business address, refund policy and subscription cancellation conditions, privacy policy, promotion/trial terms visible where customers opt in, HTTPS/PCI statement, public pages. Statement descriptor should match the business name (5–22 chars). Real-estate CRM SaaS is not on Stripe's restricted list.
6. **B2B law and venue**: choice of Spanish law valid (Rome I art. 3). Domestic venue: LEC art. 54.2 makes express submission clauses in adhesion contracts invalid even B2B, so the terms name the Spanish courts under the applicable rules rather than a city. Cross-border: Brussels I bis art. 25 allows a choice-of-court in durable electronic form. An autónomo contracting for the agency is not a consumer (TRLGDCU art. 3): no 14-day withdrawal. LCGC arts. 5 and 7: general conditions bind only with express notice and a real chance to read them — the sign-in line plus permanently available terms is the minimum; a checkbox is stronger. No Spanish-language mandate for B2B pages; publish ES + EN and say which prevails (English, for now).
7. **AI features**: name the LLM provider as recipient/sub-processor with its transfer tool. AEPD "Orientaciones sobre IA agéntica" (Feb 2026): when personal data goes to another entity's generative-AI service, inform both the users and the people the data is about; such providers act as processors. EDPB Opinion 28/2024 and the ChatGPT Taskforce report: tell users outputs may be wrong. AI Act art. 50 (from 2 Aug 2026) does not catch a draft-then-agent-sends flow, but keep the inaccuracy disclaimer and "agent reviews before sending". OpenAI API: no training by default, abuse logs ≤30 days, EU residency available; Anthropic: no training on customer content, deletion within 30 days.
8. **Transfers**: DPF adequacy decision (EU) 2023/1795 stands (Latombe dismissed 3 Sep 2025; appeal C-703/25 P pending): rely on DPF where certified, keep SCCs (Decision (EU) 2021/914) as fallback. Certified: Google LLC, Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.), Stripe (self-reported). Vercel: DPA relies on SCCs, DPF status unverified. Not found on the DPF list: OpenAI, Anthropic, Supabase (all SCC-based DPAs). Keep the Supabase project in an EU region.
