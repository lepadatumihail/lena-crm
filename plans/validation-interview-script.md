# Lena — 5-Agent Validation Interview Script

**Goal:** test the single assumption the entire positioning rests on —
> *Cross-agency client fragmentation is an **acute, switch-worthy** pain for independent Costa del Sol agents — not a tolerable annoyance they've already absorbed into WhatsApp + memory.*

**The one decision this informs:** do you commit the product's headline + name to **multi-agency consolidation** ("every client, every agency, one place"), or **pivot the spear** to multilingual AI follow-ups + cold-lead surfacing? (Per `plans/gtm-strategy.md` §8.)

**Do this BEFORE** rewriting the landing page or committing a new name.

---

## The 5 rules (read before every call — they prevent false positives)

This is *The Mom Test*. Break these and you'll get flattering, useless data.

1. **Talk about THEIR life, not your idea.** Don't describe Lena at all until the interview is over. If they ask "what are you building?", say *"a tool for agents — but I'll bore you with it after, I'd rather hear how you actually work right now."*
2. **Ask about the past, not the future.** "Tell me about the last time…" → gold. "Would you…?" / "Do you think you'd use…?" → garbage.
3. **Ask for specifics and stories, not opinions.** "Walk me through your last deal" beats "is tracking deals hard?"
4. **Shut up and let them talk.** After you ask, count to three. The pain is usually in the second half of their answer.
5. **Dig into emotion and workarounds.** When they sigh, get annoyed, or mention a hack they built (a colour-coded spreadsheet, a second phone) — *that's* the signal. Ask "show me" and "what did that cost you?"

**Anti-goals:** no pitching, no feature-listing, no "we're thinking of building X — useful?", no leading ("don't you hate it when…?").

---

## Recruiting (who + the ask)

- **Who:** 5 *independent / solo* agents on the Costa del Sol who **work across more than one agency** (collaboration/shared-listing agents — the ICP). Use your warm contacts; ask each for one more name at the end.
- **Length:** 25–35 min. In person (coffee) > video > phone. Record audio *with permission* so you can listen, not scribble.
- **The ask (WhatsApp, Mom-Test compliant — note it does NOT sell):**
  > *"Hola [name] — I'm digging into how independent agents here actually run their day-to-day (clients, follow-ups, working across agencies). Not selling anything — genuinely trying to learn from people who do this well. Could I buy you a coffee for 30 min this week and pick your brain? Would really value it."*

## The script

> Square brackets = your intent / what to listen for. Don't read those aloud.

### 0. Warm-up (2 min)
- "Tell me how you got into real estate here — are you on your own, or with an agency?"
- "Roughly how many active buyers and sellers are you juggling right now?"
- "Where are your leads coming from these days?" *[portals / referrals / agencies — sets context]*

### 1. The workflow (the spine — 8 min)
- "Walk me through the **last deal you closed**, start to finish. Who else was involved?" *[LISTEN: did they collaborate with other agencies? commission split? a 'central manager' agent? This reveals the cross-agency reality without you naming it.]*
- "When you're working a buyer, how do you find them properties — just your agency's listings, or do you pull from others?" *[gauges how multi-agency their flow really is]*
- "How many **different agencies** have you collaborated with on deals in the **last 6 months**?" *[THE COUNT. 3+ = structurally fragmented. 0–1 = the wedge is weak for them.]*

### 2. The killer test (5 min) — do this, don't just ask
- "Can I ask you to **pull up whatever you use to track clients right now**? I'd love to see how you actually do it." *[Get them to SHOW you — spreadsheet / WhatsApp / Inmovilla / notebook. Observe, don't judge.]*
- "Show me — if I asked for **every client you're working with [Agency X]** on, could you find them quickly?" *[THE TEST. Watch them try. Friction here = the pain is real. Smooth = it isn't.]*
- "Has a client or deal ever **slipped through the cracks** because it was split across places or agencies? Tell me what happened." *[Want a real past-tense story. A vivid, annoyed story = GREEN. "Not really" = RED. Lost commission = jackpot.]*
- "Have you built any **system or trick** to keep it all straight?" *[Workarounds = proof of real pain.]*

### 3. Follow-ups & cold leads (4 min)
- "Walk me through a normal morning — how do you decide who to follow up with?"
- "When's the last time you realised you'd **forgotten to follow up** with someone? What happened?" *[validates the 'Needs attention' wedge + the pivot option]*

### 4. Multilingual (3 min)
- "What languages do your clients speak? How do you handle writing to them?"
- "Do you ever **put off messaging** a client because of the language, or not knowing what to say?" *[validates AI-follow-up wedge — the #1 pivot if consolidation is weak]*

### 5. Tools & money (4 min — WTP without pitching)
- "What do you use to manage all this today?" *[their real stack]*
- "Have you ever tried a proper CRM? What happened?" *[why they churned — validates anti-bloat positioning]*
- "What do you **pay for each month** for your work — portals, MLS, leads, anything?" *[Resales €150? Idealista? This is your willingness-to-pay read. Never ask 'would you pay for mine.']*

### 6. Wrap (2 min)
- "If you had a magic wand for the **admin side** of your job, what's the first thing you'd fix?" *[unprompted #1 priority — does consolidation come up on its own?]*
- "Who else do you know who works **across agencies** like you, that I should talk to?" *[next interview]*

## Scoring — turn 5 conversations into a decision

After each call, score the **primary assumption** plus the two pivot candidates.

**A. Consolidation pain (the spear):**
| | Signal |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **GREEN** | Collaborates with **3+ agencies**; clients scattered across WhatsApp/notes/multiple tools; **a real story** of something slipping; built a manual workaround. |
| 🟡 **AMBER** | 2–3 agencies but "I manage fine"; mild annoyance, no real cost, no story. |
| 🔴 **RED** | Works ~1 agency / clients all in one place / deals don't overlap. |

**B. Follow-up / cold-lead pain** — 🟢 if they have a forgotten-follow-up story they regret.
**C. Multilingual pain** — 🟢 if they admit delaying/dreading client messages across languages.

### Decision rule
- **≥ 3 of 5 GREEN on A** → **commit** the consolidation headline + name. Build the founding cohort around it.
- **≤ 2 GREEN on A** → **pivot the spear** to whichever of B / C scored highest; demote consolidation to a supporting feature. (B → lead on "never drop a follow-up"; C → lead on "AI writes every client in their language.")
- **Either way:** the money question (§5) tells you if €19–29 is in range vs what they already pay.

> Be honest in scoring — the whole point is to let real agents *kill* a wrong assumption cheaply, before you spend weeks building a landing page and brand around it.

## After the interview (NOT during): the soft design-partner ask

Only if the call went well and they fit the ICP:
> *"This was super helpful. I'm actually building a simple tool for exactly this — for agents who work across several agencies. I'm hand-picking a small founding group to shape it with me. No pressure at all — would you want an early look?"*

If yes → that's a warm lead into the **Founding Agent** cohort (see `plans/invite-referral-spec.md`). If they hesitate, that's *also* data. Don't push.

## Cheat sheet (have this open on the call)

1. Last deal — who else involved?
2. **How many agencies in the last 6 months?**
3. **Show me your client tracking. Find me everyone with [Agency X].**
4. **Story: ever lost a deal across the cracks?**
5. Any workaround you built?
6. Last forgotten follow-up?
7. Put off messaging over language?
8. What do you pay monthly now?
9. Magic wand for admin — first fix?
10. Who else should I talk to?

*Rules: past not future · stories not opinions · don't pitch · shut up and listen.*
